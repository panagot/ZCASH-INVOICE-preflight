# PRODUCT.md

## Register
product

## Product purpose
Merchant preflight for Zcash invoices aimed at BTCPay-adjacent checkout: build/parse ZIP-321 URIs, catch fatal mistakes before a customer pays, hand off a clean pay page.

## Users
- BTCPay / self-hosted merchants accepting ZEC
- Zcash wallet/plugin maintainers reviewing the companion tool
- ZCG reviewers evaluating LOI-ready demos

## Tone
Utilitarian. POS terminal, not marketing site. Honest about mocks. No hype.

## Anti-references
- Purple SaaS dashboards
- Generic “AI crypto” dark gold glow
- Another indexer/observatory status page
- Fake “live chain” claims

## Strategic principles
1. One job: invoice hygiene before send
2. Fail loud on ZIP-321 violations (e.g. memo + transparent)
3. Companion to BTCPay Zcash — never claim to replace it
4. Demo must be reviewable in 10 minutes
